Reorganize Home, Explore, and creator-profile content filters with one focused navigation cleanup.

Important: Do not modify or regress authentication, Taste Match calculation, Tune Your Taste, Taste Seal, Edit Profile, profile-photo crop, upload, publishing, visibility permissions, Follow, Subscribe, outfit details, collections, or existing creator data.

1. Simplify Home

Remove the global category strip from Home:

* All
* Fashion & Outfits
* Travel
* Places
* Restaurants
* Personal Care
* Health & Fitness
* Decor
* Books
* Vlogs

Replace it with three simple feed tabs:

* For You
* Following
* Subscribed

Behavior:

* For You: keep the current personalized taste-led feed.
* Following: show Edits from creators the authenticated user follows.
* Subscribed: show content from creators for whom the viewer has an active subscription, using the existing subscription/demo state. Do not build real payment processing in this task.
* If Following or Subscribed is empty, show a refined empty state with an action linking to Explore.
* Keep the existing “Follow the taste, not the numbers.” Home identity.
* Do not display follower counts.

2. Keep global discovery categories in Explore

Keep the global category filters in Explore because they are used to discover creators, places, and Edits across TASTEKIN.

Do not duplicate the Home feed tabs inside Explore.

Preserve:

* Best Match
* New
* Global category filters
* Real Taste Match scores and shared-taste reasons
* Current authenticated-user exclusion from their own Explore results

3. Add creator-specific filters inside Profile

On every creator profile, keep the main tabs:

* Edits
* Collections
* About

Inside the Edits tab, add a horizontal filter row:

* All
* Only the categories that creator has actually published content in

Example for Fheed:

* All
* Fashion & Outfits
* Travel
* Places

Requirements:

* Generate the profile category filters dynamically from the creator’s published Edits.
* Do not show categories with no content.
* Selecting a category filters only that creator’s Edits.
* Keep Public and Subscribers Only visibility behavior unchanged.
* Locked subscriber content may remain visible as a blurred locked preview but must not be unlocked without an active subscription.
* Use human-readable labels such as “Daily Routine,” never raw database keys like DailyRoutine.
* If the creator has content in only one category, the filter row may remain hidden to keep the profile clean.
* The same profile filters must work in owner view and visitor view.

4. Mobile layout

At 390px iPhone width:

* Home feed tabs must fit cleanly without horizontal clipping.
* Explore category filters may scroll horizontally.
* Creator-profile category filters may scroll horizontally.
* Keep the bottom navigation and safe-area behavior unchanged.

5. Acceptance tests

Verify:

* Home no longer shows the long global category list.
* Home shows For You, Following, and Subscribed.
* Explore still shows Best Match, New, and global categories.
* Fheed’s profile Edits tab shows only categories in which Fheed has published Edits.
* Selecting a profile category correctly filters the grid.
* Visitor and owner views behave consistently.
* Locked content remains protected.
* Taste Match, profile editing, crop, upload, Publish, Follow, Subscribe, and Taste Seal still work.
* All existing tests and production builds pass.

Do not make unrelated design, billing, data-model, or architecture changes.

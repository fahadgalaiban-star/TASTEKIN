2. Create “Tune your taste”

Create a mobile-first “Tune your taste” screen accessible from:

* The settings/sliders menu
* Tapping the Taste Match pill on the user’s own profile

Allow authenticated users to:

* Select and deselect top-level categories
* Select specific taste tags
* Save their taste profile
* Return later and update it

Persist selections securely to the authenticated user.

The user’s private taste selections must not expose date of birth, email, identity documents, or other private profile information.

3. Calculate a real Taste Match

Replace every hard-coded or random “92% Taste Match” value with a deterministic server-side calculation between the authenticated viewer and the viewed creator.

For this first version use a transparent weighted-overlap calculation:

* 35% category overlap
* 65% specific taste-tag overlap

Calculate each overlap from the shared selections divided by the combined unique selections. Round the final result to a whole percentage.

Requirements:

* No random scores
* No hard-coded score for Fheed
* The score must change when the viewer updates their taste
* The result must persist correctly after refresh
* Calculate or validate the score server-side
* Use existing caching only if it is safely invalidated whenever either taste profile changes
* If the viewer has not completed enough preferences, show “Build your taste profile” instead of a fake percentage

4. Explain the match

When a visitor taps a creator’s Taste Match pill, open an elegant mobile bottom sheet titled:

“Why you match”

Show:

* The real percentage
* Up to three strongest shared tastes
* A short sentence generated from deterministic templates, not AI

Example:

“You both prefer quiet luxury, walkable cities and boutique hotels.”

Include a “Tune my taste” action for the viewer.

Do not expose internal calculation details or private information.

5. Improve Explore around taste

On the Explore screen:

* Show verified and eligible creators with their real Taste Match score
* Add two simple sorting options: “Best Match” and “New”
* Default authenticated users to “Best Match”
* Display one or two shared-taste reasons on each creator card
* Do not show follower or following counts
* Do not rank creators by follower count
* Do not change the existing TASTEKIN visual identity

6. Seed and fallback behavior

Update seed/demo taste profiles so different creators produce meaningfully different match scores.

Do not overwrite Fheed’s real profile edits, profile photo, published Edits, collections, verification status, or authenticated ownership mapping.

For signed-out visitors, do not invent a personal match. Show “Sign in to discover your Taste Match.”
